Clay & Color: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Clay & Color is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information, and it has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

Your sculptures, shelf, photos, in-game channel and settings are saved only on your own device (in the browser's storage for the web version, or the app's storage on Android and iOS). They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

The in-game channel

The channel, its followers and their comments are part of the game and run entirely on your device. Nothing you post there leaves your device.

Sharing photos

If you choose to share a photo, the game hands it to your device's own share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share it with.

Hosting

The web version is served by GitHub Pages and itch.io, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. The game itself sends nothing to them while you play.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the game's itch.io page: kiristineryana.itch.io/clayandcolor.
